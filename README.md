# CloudBarber 💈

**Modernizing the Walk-in Experience.**

CloudBarber is a web application designed to solve the classic barbershop pain point: **uncertain wait times**. Traditional barbershops often operate on a walk-in basis, leading to crowded waiting rooms and frustrated customers who have no idea how long they'll be waiting.

CloudBarber solves this by providing a **live digital view** of seat availability. Owners can manage their shop status in real-time, and customers can check the queue from their phone before they even head out.

## ✨ Key Features (MVP v1)

### For Shop Owners ✂️

- **Digital Shop Setup**: Create a profile with your **Store Name** and **Opening Times**.
- **Seat Management**: Add "seats" to your digital shop to match your physical chairs.
- **Live Status**: Toggle seats as "Occupied" or "Free" with a click.
- **Shareable Link**: Generate a public link for your customers.
- **Basic Analytics**: (v1 Perk) View simple stats on shop activity.

### For Customers 🧔

- **Real-Time Availability**: Instantly see how many seats are free or taken.
- **Wait Time Estimates**: viewing estimated wait times based on current occupancy.
- **No App Required**: Access the shop status via a simple web link.
